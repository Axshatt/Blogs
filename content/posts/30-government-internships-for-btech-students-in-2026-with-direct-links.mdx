If you're a BTech student in India searching for a government internship in 2026, this is the only list you need.

No Internshala scrolling. No outdated PDFs. Just 30 verified government internship opportunities — with official direct links — across ISRO, DRDO, PSUs, research institutions, IIT programs, and ministry schemes.

Whether you're at an IIT, NIT, or a tier-2 university, most of these programs have no college-tier filter. What matters is your CGPA, your project work, and how early you apply.

## Why Government Internships Matter for BTech Students

A government internship certificate from DRDO, ISRO, BARC, or NTPC carries weight that most private internship certificates simply don't. Recruiters at PSUs, core engineering companies, and research institutes actively look for candidates who have worked in real government-grade environments.

Beyond the resume value, you get access to actual labs, working scientists as mentors, and the kind of projects that don't exist in any startup or IT company.

Here's the full list — broken down by category.

---

## Category 1: Defence & Space (Dream Tier)

These are the most prestigious and competitive. Apply early — most run on a rolling basis or have specific windows.

---

### 1. ISRO – Main Internship and Student Project Trainee Scheme

ISRO runs internship and project trainee programs across all its centres for UG, PG, and PhD students in science and technology. There is no single centralized portal — each ISRO centre has its own application process and deadlines.

**Stipend:** No monetary stipend, but an official ISRO certificate on completion.
**Duration:** Minimum 45 days.
**Eligibility:** BTech/BE, MTech/ME students with minimum 60% aggregate or CGPA 6.32/10. Indian nationals only.

**Apply here:** [isro.gov.in/Internship.html](https://www.isro.gov.in/Internship.html)

---

### 2. ISRO – IIRS Dehradun External Student Internship

The Indian Institute of Remote Sensing (IIRS), a unit of ISRO based in Dehradun, offers a structured external student internship twice a year. Applications close on 31st October and 31st March every year.

**Stipend:** No stipend.
**Duration:** Minimum 45 days, maximum 1 year.
**Eligibility:** UG/PG/PhD students with 60%+ marks. Indian nationals only.

**Apply here:** [iirs.gov.in – External Student Internship](https://www.iirs.gov.in/content/external-student-internship)

---

### 3. ISRO – LPSC Internship (Bengaluru / Thiruvananthapuram)

The Liquid Propulsion Systems Centre (LPSC) offers internships for BE/BTech and ME/MTech students. For the October 2026–January 2027 block, applications open from 1st August to 31st August 2026.

**Stipend:** No stipend.
**Duration:** Block-based, typically 3 months.
**Eligibility:** BE/BTech, ME/MTech students from recognized institutions.

**Apply here:** [lpsc.gov.in – Internship](https://www.lpsc.gov.in/Internship.html)

---

### 4. ISRO – SDSC SHAR Sriharikota Internship

Satish Dhawan Space Centre accepts internship and project training requests from students. You apply directly via email to the Head of HRM at least one month before your preferred start date, along with a bonafide certificate from your Principal or HoD.

**Stipend:** No stipend.
**Duration:** As per academic requirement.
**Eligibility:** Science and Technology students from recognized universities.

**Apply here:** [shar.gov.in – Internship](https://www.shar.gov.in/sdscshar/internship.jsp)

---

### 5. ISRO – START 2026 Programme

ISRO's Space Technology and Research (START) program is a special initiative for academic institutions, allowing students and faculty to collaborate on ISRO-relevant research areas.

**Stipend:** Varies by project.

### 23. NATS – National Apprenticeship Training Scheme

Honestly the most underrated scheme on this entire list. NATS places fresh engineering and diploma graduates as apprentices at PSUs like HAL, BHEL, ISRO, DRDO, BEL, BARC, NTPC, ONGC, and Indian Railways for 12 months with a Government of India-recognized certificate. Many high performers get converted to permanent roles after completion.

**Stipend:** ₹9,000–₹12,300/month for 12 months.
**Duration:** 1 year.
**Eligibility:** Fresh engineering or diploma graduates.

**Apply here:** [nats.education.gov.in](https://nats.education.gov.in)

## How to Actually Get Selected

**Start earlier than everyone else.** DRDO, IIT research programs, and ISRO centres fill up fast. Most students apply two weeks before the deadline — you should apply two months before.

**CGPA matters, but projects matter more.** A 7.5 CGPA with a real GitHub project beats an 8.5 CGPA with nothing to show. Build something before you apply.

**For NATS, apply to multiple vacancies simultaneously.** New vacancies are posted continuously. Check the portal every few days. HAL, BHEL, ISRO, DRDO all regularly post NATS vacancies.

**For IIT programs, your SOP is everything.** Be specific about what you want to work on, why that faculty's research interests you, and what you bring. Generic SOPs get ignored.

**For DRDO, check lab-wise.** DMSRDE Kanpur, INMAS Delhi, DIPAS Delhi — these labs have separate internship notifications. Don't just email DRDO headquarters.

**Keep your documents ready in advance.** Most applications need a latest marksheet, bonafide certificate, NOC from HoD, resume, and statement of purpose. Keep all of these updated and ready as a single PDF before applications open.

## FAQ

**Can students from tier-2 or state universities apply?**
Yes. NATS, AICTE Portal, DRDO, NTPC, BHEL, ONGC, MEA, and all IIT research programs accept students from any recognized institution. There is no IIT/NIT-only filter anywhere on this list.

**Which government internship has the highest stipend?**
NeGD/Digital India pays ₹20,000/month for 6 months. IIT Madras pays ₹15,000/month. TIFR VSRP pays ₹10,000–₹15,000/month. NATS pays ₹9,000–₹12,300/month for 12 months, making it the highest total earning across the year.

**Are ISRO and DRDO internships paid?**
ISRO internships are generally unpaid but give you an ISRO certificate. DRDO has paid internship schemes across specific labs at ₹5,000–₹30,000/month — check the DRDO vacancies page for current active notifications.

**What is the best government internship for CS/IT BTech students?**
DRDO CS/IT labs, NeGD/Digital India, NIC, ISRO CS-related centres like ISAC, TIFR Computer Science, and IIT research programs are the best options for CS/IT students in 2026.

**Is NATS worth it?**
Yes. It is the most underrated scheme on this list. Twelve months of real PSU work experience, a Government of India certificate, ₹9,000–₹12,300/month stipend, and many high performers convert to permanent roles. Apply at [nats.education.gov.in](https://nats.education.gov.in).
